✦ How It All Started

The Sisíológe Story

Sisíológe. Say it slowly. It is Yoruba for the woman who knows and loves fashion. The woman who walks into a room and the accessories do the talking before she even opens her mouth. That woman is who we are here for.

But getting here was not a straight line and honestly we are glad for that. The brand you know today was originally called LIORÉ. A different name, a different era. We were figuring it out, building, learning, pivoting. And then we had to restructure and that restructure became everything.

We started with thrifted clothes, moved into jewellery, tried different things and slowly started to understand what we were actually building. In life there is always room for trial and error. The error is never the end. It is just the edit.

And eventually, out of all of that, sisíológe was birthed. A curated space for bags, accessories, caps and everything in between. Handpicked. Quality checked. Made for the sisí who does not settle.

We are here to stay. And we are just getting started.

"Sisíológe was not planned. It was earned."

Opemipo Fapohunda, Founder

✦ The Founder

Meet Opemipo

Lagos born. Chronically online. Always curating. Opemipo Fapohunda is the founder of sisíológe and honestly she is the brand. Creative professional by day, accessories obsessive always.

She is the kind of person who notices your bag before she notices anything else. Who knows the difference between a good find and a great one. Who built sisíológe because she could not find a space that curated the way she curates.

She fits every era. Gen Z energy, millennial hustle, gen alpha vision. She is not just keeping up with the times. She is setting the pace.
