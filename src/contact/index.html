✦ Quick Answers

FAQs

What are your delivery days?

We deliver every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. Same day delivery is available for orders placed before 9AM on a delivery day.

How much is delivery in Lagos?

Lagos delivery ranges from ₦2,500 to ₦6,500 depending on your area. Select your area at checkout to see your exact fee.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes! We deliver nationwide via Moveit.ng. Delivery takes 2-5 working days. Prices start from ₦5,000.

Do you have a new customer discount?

What is your return policy?

All sales are final. If there is an issue with your order please contact us immediately on WhatsApp and we will resolve it!
