I honestly hesitated about having a blog section, but I have always wanted to do this. Like, for years. Every time I come across someone's blog, the personal ones, the kind where you can actually hear the person talking and not performing, I'd think, "one day." And then life happens, and the idea just sits there quietly, waiting for me to come back to it.

I think the real reason I never started is because I kept waiting for the "right" reason. Like I needed a big, polished mission statement before I could type a single word. But honestly? There's no perfect reason to start writing. There's just the moment you decide you're done waiting.

So here we are. And today, that moment happens to line up with something else too.

I keep saying to myself, sisíológe website is launching. Well, sort of.

We're not 100% live yet, we're at about 80%. Think of today as a *PRELAUNCH*. The site is up, the shop works, you can genuinely buy any piece right now if something catches your eye. But we're still adding pieces, still tightening a few things, still finding our footing. Consider yourself one of the first people seeing this before it's fully polished.

This blog will cover a bit of everything. *Fashion, the Sisíológe journey* (the real one, not just the highlight reel), random life thoughts, maybe even some of my unbothered hot takes because I don't hold back when I have an opinion. This is not me performing for an audience, but actually letting you into the parts of building something that nobody shows you.

If you've been following Sisíológe from the LIORÉ days, you already know this brand has never followed a straight line. We started with thrifted pieces, we tried jewelries, we pivoted, we restructured, and somehow out of all that mess, sisíológe was born. A name, a vision, a whole identity that actually makes sense now.

If you shop today, thank you. Truly. You're not just buying a piece, you're part of the very first chapter of this thing. And if you spot something that feels off or broken, please tell me. I would rather know now than pretend everything's perfect when it isn't.

There's a little something for you too. Use code OMOGE at checkout for 25% off, but don't sit on it, it disappears in 24 hours.

So welcome, Sisí. However you found your way here, Instagram, a friend, randomly clicking a link, I'm glad you're here. This is going to be a space where I write the way I actually talk. No filter, no fake polish, just real.

Let's see where this goes together. 

— Ope of Sisíológe
